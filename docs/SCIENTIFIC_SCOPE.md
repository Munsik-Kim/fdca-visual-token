# Scientific scope

FDCA's defensible contribution is a specified measurement contract: categorical onset probability, natural split seeds, split-conditional consequence, a common propagation kernel, audited coupling/RNG, and paired-block inference. Isolated-shock sensitivity and error propagation have close precedents. The study has not demonstrated superior prospective prediction or precision/restoration selection against strong cost-matched baselines.

The visual intervention compares one FP32-versus-W8 transition from a common reference anchor state, then runs both branches with the same W8 kernel. It uses fixed 32-step Halton `randomize=False` scheduling and independent coordinatewise conditional maximal coupling. Anchor h is a conditional batch split probability under that coupling, not a binomial split frequency or a persistent-path survival hazard. Anchor probabilities cannot be accumulated into a persistent-quantization CDF or expected whole-generation discrepancy.

Across equally weighted blocks, H is mean(h), M_equal is mean(m), R is mean(h*m), and M_split=R/H when H>0. R differs from H*M_equal by the population covariance with 1/n normalization. h=0 contributes zero to R without fabricating an observed conditional m. The symmetric incidence/consequence attribution is computed within each paired block before averaging; it is arithmetic, not causal mediation.

Primary inference pairs .5 and .3 within Tiny CFG3 W8 n=48 and Small CFG3 W8 n=40. CFG1 n=16 and W4 n=24 overlap their parent panels. Matched Tiny n=24 / Small n=40 results are separate diagnostic interventions. Conditional repeats are not independent blocks. Compact files omit complete class/seed manifests: neither class-cluster resampling nor independent exhaustive predecessor-disjointness verification is available. Block bootstrap assumes independent equally weighted class–seed units in the selected panels; repeated seeds within one class are not automatically pseudoreplication.

Rho is target unresolved fraction, not quantization strength. Larger rho is earlier; target and attained fractions differ. In .3→.5, batch c changes 14→8. A lower batch probability does not imply lower per-coordinate TV; remaining horizon, states, coordinates and seed composition also change. Matched timing controls recorded seed identity for its purpose, without isolating a pure causal time effect or independently exposing the complete coordinate identities.

The results have distinct assumptions. The stable-set bound uses finite nonnegative additive costs with zero self-cost and bounded diameters on the future-stable state domain. The product split law additionally uses product categorical marginals and independent coordinate couplings. The DAG theorem requires a fixed branch-independent monotone commit schedule, the stated coordinate maximal coupling, and global influence suprema over the needed branch and hybrid states. Strict finite block triangularity gives nilpotency and a finite inverse without contraction. Finite measured profiles are not a global certificate. Hamming/token bounds do not imply an LPIPS bound without additional verified decoder/metric assumptions and constants. See the [proof ledger](PROOF_LEDGER_v1.0.1.md).

LPIPS/DINO quantify paired output discrepancy, not human perception, quality or preference. The evidence concerns two checkpoints in one architecture family, one schedule and the declared coupling; it does not cover arbitrary rewriting/confidence samplers, diffusion, new architectures, coupling independence, universal opposite h/m directions, or all-rho monotonicity. The original 20,000/higher headline inference and new 100,000/linear post-hoc robustness analysis are explicitly separated.

Frozen source snapshots resolve quantizer implementation to per-output-channel weight-only symmetric RTN fake quantization and document the P2P per-tensor plan deviation. Runtime scale/weight tensors were not reattested; packed-kernel speed/VRAM benefits were not measured. Preregistration YAML existence and internal source hashes do not establish an independent external pre-outcome timestamp.

Negative P05R/P1M findings, incomplete P2A and subsequent P2B audit closure, and W4's stress-only scope remain part of the scientific history. [Follow-up experiments](FOLLOWUP_PLAN.md), including restoration policies and human studies, are explicitly NOT RUN. No new model inference or GPU experiment was conducted for v1.0.1.
