# Scientific scope

Theorems are stated under explicit product-law, hybrid-state and monotone schedule assumptions. Empirical results are limited to Halton `randomize=False`, conditional-form coordinatewise coupling, one-step natural FP32-vs-W8 seeds followed by a common W8 suffix, two checkpoints in one model family, and operational paired metrics. Measured finite-panel influence is not a global certificate. Single-shock propagation and persistent approximation are distinct estimands.
