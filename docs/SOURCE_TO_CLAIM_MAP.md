The independent-unit assumption is across the selected blocks. The public compact CSVs do not carry the complete class_id/base_seed manifest, so this revision cannot exhaustively verify class–seed uniqueness, predecessor disjointness, or class clustering from public inputs. The historical records state that new/disjoint blocks were selected; that statement is distinguished from a current independent manifest audit. Multiple seeds of one class do not automatically constitute pseudoreplication. The block bootstrap describes the chosen block population under its independence assumption; it does not establish class-level population inference.

P2P's contrast was selected after preceding P2N evidence, before its recorded new-block panel. P3S's recorded power plan used half the P2P difference and chose 40 among 32/40/48 candidates. Repository YAMLs and internal frozen hashes document the plans, but publication after execution supplies no independent pre-experiment timestamp certification. The new 100,000-resample linear-quantile calculations are explicitly post hoc and separate from the original 20,000-resample higher-quantile headline analysis.
